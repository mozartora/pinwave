Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Overview

PinPulse is built local-first. What you do in the app stays on your device unless a feature explicitly needs the network. This policy describes what the app stores, what it fetches, and what it never does.

What is stored locally on your device

  • Your saved trends and collections
  • Your preferences (appearance, notifications, subscription plan)
  • Your recent searches

This data lives in a local database on your device. In the current build there is no PinPulse account server at all — nothing above is uploaded.

What is fetched over the network

  • Trend and Pin content from the official Pinterest API, only after you connect your Pinterest account via OAuth
  • Clearly-labeled demo content while Pinterest is not connected

Pinterest data handling

When you connect Pinterest, you authorize PinPulse on Pinterest's own page. PinPulse receives a limited access token — never your password — and requests only the scopes it needs. Pinterest content is cached briefly with expiration and deleted automatically; it is not stored long-term. Tokens are kept in encrypted storage and are never sent to analytics services or third parties.

What PinPulse never does

  • Sell or share your data with third parties
  • Send Pinterest tokens to analytics providers
  • Scrape Pinterest or use undocumented endpoints
  • Build an image repository from Pinterest content

Your controls

Disconnect Pinterest at any time from Settings → Pinterest. Remove all local data with Settings → Delete account. Uninstalling the app removes everything stored on the device.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the developer via the contact listed on the app's store listing.
